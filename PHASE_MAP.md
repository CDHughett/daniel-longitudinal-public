# Phase Map

The Daniel Longitudinal Study is organized into sequential operational phases.

Each phase represents a **different system objective and constraint environment**.

Phase advancement is governed by the criteria defined in  
`PHASE_DECLARATION_CRITERIA.md`.

---

# Phase 0 — System Awakening (Historical)

Initial recognition that biological trajectory required intervention.

Characteristics:

- unstable training architecture
- inconsistent recovery signals
- early experimentation
- foundational measurement exposure

This phase produced the **initial awareness and baseline observations** that motivated the structured archive.

Artifacts from this period exist but are not the primary focus of the public archive.

---

# Phase 1 — Repair, Purification, Stability

Primary objective: restore baseline biological integrity.

Characteristics:

- removal of destabilizing inputs
- recovery normalization
- metabolic repair
- training architecture stabilization
- consistent measurement exposure

Outcome:

- stable physiological signals
- repeatable recovery patterns
- reliable measurement continuity

Phase 1 established the **biological platform required for structured load integration**.

---

# Phase 2 — Load Integration and Structural Expansion

Primary objective: introduce progressive load while maintaining system stability.

Characteristics:

- structured training architecture
- controlled workload expansion
- recovery monitoring integration
- endurance and strength convergence
- biological response observation under repeated stimulus

Phase 2 focuses on **building structural capacity without destabilizing recovery systems**.

---

# Phase 3 — External Capability Demonstration (Reserved)

Primary objective: translate internally stabilized capacity into externally observable outputs only if prior phase criteria are retrospectively satisfied.

Potential characteristics:

- public capability demonstrations
- expanded output environments
- external observation and measurement
- continued artifact capture

This phase is not active.

It is preserved as a future structural category, not a prediction or declared outcome.

---

# Phase Transition Principles

Phase advancement requires:

- sustained artifact continuity
- stable recovery signals
- evidence of system adaptation
- compliance with governance constraints

Phase declarations occur **retrospectively**, not aspirationally.

---

# Archive Principle

Phases describe **system state**, not personal narrative.
