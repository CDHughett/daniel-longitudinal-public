# Phase Map

The Daniel Longitudinal Study is organized into sequential operational phases.

Each phase represents a distinct:

- system objective
- constraint environment
- evidence requirement
- interpretation boundary

Phase advancement is governed by:

[`PHASE_DECLARATION_CRITERIA.md`](PHASE_DECLARATION_CRITERIA.md)

Phase language follows this hierarchy:

```text
Phase
  → operating substate
    → candidate characteristic
      → accumulated transition evidence
        → retrospective declaration
```

A candidate characteristic is not a declared substate.

An operating substate is not automatically a new phase.

A phase transition exists only after retrospective review satisfies the applicable declaration criteria.

# Phase 0 — Baseline Reconstruction

## Status

Historical.

## Primary Objective

Recognize the need for intervention and establish the initial observational baseline.

## Characteristics

- unstable training architecture
- inconsistent recovery signals
- early experimentation
- incomplete measurement continuity
- limited protocol governance
- foundational exposure to biological and performance measurement
- recognition that the existing trajectory required deliberate intervention

## Archive Role

Phase 0 produced:

- initial awareness
- baseline observations
- early source artifacts
- the need for a structured archive
- the first distinction between subjective impression and recorded evidence

Artifacts from this period remain relevant for historical context but are not the primary governed observation layer.

## Historical Aliases

Historical documents may refer to Phase 0 as:

- System Awakening
- Baseline Reconstruction
- Pre-Firmware Baseline Epoch

These terms refer to the same broad historical phase unless a specific document clearly defines a narrower local meaning.

- metabolic and behavioral stabilization
- repeatable training exposure
- improving measurement continuity

## Outcome

Phase 1 established:

- more stable physiological signals
- repeatable recovery patterns
- reliable execution
- improved measurement continuity

## Historical Aliases

# Phase 2 — Load Integration

## Status

Active.

## Primary Objective

Introduce and stabilize structured load while preserving recovery, behavioral reliability, and system coherence.

## Characteristics

- stable B1 aerobic execution
- structured Load Integration
- strength and endurance compatibility
- controlled workload exposure
- recovery monitoring
- mechanical-signal observation
- repeated protocol execution without compensatory escalation
- adaptation under ordinary-life variability
- increasing movement control and optionality
- observation of reduced operator overhead
- continued biological and performance measurement

Phase 2 focuses on building structural capacity without destabilizing the recovery platform established during Phase 1.

---

## Phase 2 Operating Substates

Operating substates describe the mode in which Phase 2 is currently expressed.

They do not replace the canonical Phase 2 declaration.

### Entry and Reintegration

Possible characteristics:

- initial exposure to structured load
- reintroduction after travel or interruption
- observation of acute recovery compatibility
- confirmation that prior capacity remains accessible
- conservative execution without compensatory workload

This may occur more than once during Phase 2.

It is an operating condition rather than a new phase.

---

### Consolidation

Possible characteristics:

- repeated standard execution
- stable recovery under recurring load
- low protocol friction
- no need for graded re-entry
- preserved same-day compatibility
- reduced novelty
- diminishing need for conscious correction
- training becoming integrated into ordinary daily operation

The archive’s current operating state is most consistent with Phase 2 consolidation.

---

### Lock-In Observation

Possible characteristics:

- execution remains stable across schedule and environmental variation
- successful performance variations recur without being forced
- movement mechanics remain available under divided attention
- equipment or contextual changes do not materially degrade performance
- optional activity remains subordinate to the protocol
- available capacity exceeds required workload
- governance remains intact despite increasing capacity

“Lock-in” describes a candidate stability property within Phase 2.

It is not a second Phase 2 declaration and does not independently establish progression.

# Phase 2 Lettered Terminology

Historical and active documents may use labels such as:

```text

```

These labels require careful interpretation.

---

## Phase 2C

Phase 2C is treated as historical shorthand for a consolidation or lock-in substate within Phase 2.

It may describe:

- repeated load compatibility
- stable execution
- reduced protocol friction
- recovery preservation
- increasing structural ownership

Phase 2C is not a separate canonical phase.

Unless a dated retrospective declaration states otherwise, it should be interpreted as an operating-substate label within Phase 2.

---

## Phase 2D

Phase 2D is reserved as a possible retrospectively declared substate representing durable, portable, and low-overhead expression of the capacity built during Phase 2.

Potential characteristics may include:

- trait-like execution
- reduced operator overhead
- automatic movement mechanics
- divided-attention performance without degradation
- voluntary tempo and positional optionality
- portability across schedule or environment
- preserved recovery under ordinary perturbation
- capacity remaining available without becoming mandatory workload
- protocol governance preserved despite increased capability

These observations may accumulate as candidate transition evidence.

They do not establish Phase 2D by themselves.

---

## Phase 2D-Type Characteristics

The phrase:

```text
Phase 2D-type characteristics
```

means that an observation resembles a possible future Phase 2D criterion.

It does not mean:

- Phase 2D is active
- Phase 2 is complete
- a threshold has been met
- a transition has occurred
- the protocol should progress
- a successful behavior should be deliberately repeated as a test

Examples may include:

- ambient pull-up execution
- automatic bar contact
- conversational or divided-attention movement control
- voluntary tempo modulation
- reduced need to mentally index training
- execution surviving modest environmental or schedule variation

These remain candidate characteristics until reviewed across sufficient repetition and context.

---

## Phase 2D Declaration Boundary

A formal Phase 2D declaration would require:

- repeated evidence across time
- evidence across more than one context
- preserved recovery
- absence of meaningful mechanical degradation
- preserved protocol governance
- no reliance on forced demonstrations
- no conversion of optional capacity into mandatory workload
- retrospective review after the observation window
- explicit documentation of the declaration and supporting evidence

Until those conditions are satisfied and formally recorded:

```text
Phase 2D remains undeclared.
```

- reduced operator overhead
- portability

- model-error outcomes

One observation may be relevant without being sufficient.

---

# Candidate Characteristics

A candidate characteristic is an observation that may be relevant to a future substate or phase declaration.

Examples include:

- ambient initiation
- automatic mechanics
- reduced conscious correction
- divided-attention performance
- optional tempo control
- portability
- low-friction reintegration
- stable recovery after higher-density exposure

Candidate characteristics:

- may be recorded contemporaneously
- may recur naturally
- should not be forced for proof
- should not be converted into protocol requirements
- do not authorize phase advancement
- remain subject to retrospective review

---

# Retrospective Declaration

A phase or formal substate may be declared only after:

1. the relevant observation window has closed
2. source evidence is reviewed
3. contradictory evidence is considered
4. recovery and performance compatibility are assessed
5. applicable criteria are satisfied
6. governance remained intact
7. the declaration is documented in the appropriate archive surfaces

A retrospective declaration should identify:

- the declared state
- the effective evidence window
- the supporting evidence
- relevant limitations
- contradictory or incomplete evidence
- the prior state
- the operational consequences, if any

The declaration date and the beginning of the supporting evidence window may differ.

---

# Historical Alias Rule

Historical documents should retain their original phase language.

They should not be rewritten solely to match current terminology.

When historical wording differs from the canonical hierarchy:

- preserve the original wording
- interpret it through this phase map
- add an alias note only when confusion would otherwise result
- do not treat terminology cleanup as a biological correction
- do not infer a phase transition that was never declared

---

# Governance Boundary

Phase language must not be used to:

- create progression pressure
- justify additional workload
- convert successful variation into mandatory testing
- reward favorable biomarker outcomes
- rewrite predictions
- dismiss contradictory evidence
- transform candidate observations into declared state
- imply that the next phase is inevitable

Phase advancement remains evidence dependent and retrospective.

# Archive Principle

Phases describe system state, constraint environment, and operational objective.

They are not:

- motivational milestones
- personal-identity labels
- narrative rewards
- automatic consequences of favorable results
- substitutes for evidence

The governing principle is:

> Observe first, accumulate evidence, and declare state only in retrospect.
