Each subfolder may contain:

* README.md (context)
* PNG/JPG screenshots
* PDF reports
* checksums.txt (SHA256 integrity hashes)

---

## Naming Convention

Files follow this pattern:

YYYY-MM-DD_measurement-type.ext

Examples:

* 2026-02-12_dexa_body-comp.png
* 2026-02-12_bodpod_results.pdf

---

## Integrity Rule

Snapshots are never overwritten.
Corrections are logged via new commits only.
