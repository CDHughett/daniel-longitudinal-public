## Purpose

This report records material observations from the stated weekly window.

During an active week:

- observations may be added contemporaneously
- candidate evidence remains provisional
- unfinished trajectories remain open
- no weekly conclusion is implied

At closeout:

- interpretation becomes retrospective
- summaries must remain grounded in preserved evidence
- registered predictions remain unchanged
- phase transitions require separate governing criteria

Forward physiological predictions belong only in the formally registered model-error layer.

## Template Governance

This template governs future weekly reports beginning with the first report initialized after its adoption.

It does not require retroactive reformatting of historical reports.

Weekly reports should favor:

- material deltas
- verified metrics
- visible uncertainty
- concise interpretation
- model-error relevance
- explicit governance outcomes

They should avoid:

- repeating standing methodology
- reproducing the full protocol each week
- turning optional capacity into required workload
- presenting active observations as completed conclusions
- expanding prose without improving traceability
