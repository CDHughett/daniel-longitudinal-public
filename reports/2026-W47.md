## Primary Observation — Familiar Baseline Versus New Running Exposure

### Preserve actual B1 and LI exposure separately

Record actual completion, omission or modification of the familiar 55-minute B1 aerobic anchor and standard LI on each date. A previous day's comfortable execution is not evidence that a later session occurred. Document treadmill duration, speed, grade, measured (not merely prescribed) HR if available, reported RPE, and next-day movement cost. Distinguish measured distance from approximate/source-logged mileage.

For LI, retain separately the actual movement selection, repetitions, tempo, trap-bar plates **per side**, verified bar mass if available, grip/pull/press execution, mechanical comfort and any delayed lumbar symptoms. The historical "Early 2D" label on an LI variant does **not** declare formal Phase 2D or automatically authorize expanded loading.

Do not compensate for the planned November 16–17 testing days through artificial catch-up volume. Missing training is not a completed session; voluntary rest, symptom-based modification and provider-required restriction should remain distinct.

### Supplemental running as a separately identified observation variable

The operator has described a Monday/Wednesday/Friday post-B1 running idea. Treat that as a **candidate schedule only**, not a commitment to increase running duration, speed, grade or frequency. When a segment actually occurs, log **its own duration, speed, incline, segment-specific HR/RPE if observed, gait or movement quality, immediate symptoms, delayed 24–48-hour local response, subsequent LI quality and next-morning recovery**.

If running follows B1 within one treadmill block, keep the completed session's total duration correct while recording the running component distinctly; **never double-count the running minutes or create a second completed session without independently recorded separate exposure**. Compare repeatability with the established B1 anchor rather than assuming the novel stimulus has the same mechanical or cardiovascular demand.

A shift in running duration alongside speed or grade changes is not a controlled single-variable progression. If pain, gait changes, unusual fatigue or deterioration in execution is reported, document reduction or omission as the actual exposure; medical/provider instructions and actual symptom status take precedence over a routine target.

### Autonomic, sleep and ordinary functioning

Continue Daily Biomarkers HRV separately from the canonical overnight Sleep Log HRV, and daily resting HR separately from sleeping HR. Record measurement timing and retain discrepancies between structured fields and free-text wearable/device notes. Favor repeat observations over inference from a single low HRV reading.

Track total sleep duration and opportunity relative to the established **460-minute monitoring target**; preserve consumer sleep-stage and awakening uncertainty, particularly low-confidence REM estimates. Vivid/lucid dream recall remains subjective. Record standardized waking weight independently of condition-specific post-B1 and post-GI clearance measurements. Continue morning mood, GI/stomach, sweating, pain, EQ and material ordinary-life context from actual source entries, without inferring unavailable energy-balance or clinical recovery data.
