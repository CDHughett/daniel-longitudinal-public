## Weekly Interpretation

Week 36 is best interpreted as **consolidation with a natural interruption**, not as a regression week and not as a complete portability demonstration.

When the architecture was available, execution remained strongly preserved. Five consecutive full B1 + LI days were completed from 2026-09-07 through 2026-09-11 without evidence of recovery-directed unloading, pain-driven modification, compensatory pacing, or escalating management cost. Qualitative execution continued to be described as ambient, trait-like, and owned. The recorded 2026-09-09 pull-up set adds a contemporaneous artifact consistent with increasing movement control rather than simple task completion.

The weekend then separated capacity from exposure. Formal volume fell because ordinary travel changed access, not because the record shows an inability to continue. The correct inference is therefore narrow: the installed system remained physiologically and behaviorally stable while actual structured workload was reduced by context. Full portability remains unproven because the architecture was not executed in the travel environment.

Recovery evidence remained mixed but functional. Recurrent early-night heart-rate excursions remained visible, while late-night normalization and preserved next-day function repeatedly coexisted. That pattern strengthens the observation that wearable disturbance and demonstrated function are not perfectly coupled in the current state, while still leaving the recurrent nocturnal signal open for longitudinal attention.

Bodyweight remained broadly stable across six available morning measurements, and the lower-intake state did not correspond with a clear deterioration in mood, GI state, pain, EQ, ordinary activity, or completed training quality. The intake/bodyweight condition therefore remains observational rather than a justified intervention target.

The most informative consequence of the travel interruption moves into Week 37: whether the ordinary B1 + Load Integration cadence simply returns without compensation, negotiation, or recovery-directed modification.
