## Notes

Week 26 showed that the architecture could remain coherent while execution was constrained.

Week 27 showed that execution could return without repair.

That distinction is the central observation.

The system did not require:

- a graded re-entry
- compensatory training
- motivational reconstruction
- dietary correction
- a recovery intervention
- narrative repair
- a new protocol
- forced proof of retained capacity

It returned to standard and then expressed more control than standard completion required.

The instructional pull-up was important because attention was no longer confined entirely to performing the movement. Verbal explanation, environmental awareness, pauses, and physical execution coexisted without obvious breakdown.

The weekend observations added a related signal. Movement pace and force expression appeared increasingly selectable. Formal training remained intact while recreational aquatic work stayed comfortable and low-salience.

These may represent early Phase 2D characteristics:

- reduced operator overhead
- adaptable execution
- spare attentional capacity
- voluntary force modulation
- cross-context transfer
- preserved output after accumulated load

They do not yet establish Phase 2D.

The distinction between candidate expression and durable trait remains important.

A durable transition would require recurrence across:

- ordinary fresh sessions
- accumulated-load sessions
- schedule displacement
- divided-attention conditions
- future travel
- recovery variability
- the August objective snapshot

The appropriate response is therefore continued standard execution rather than escalation.

Week 27 was not a breakthrough week.

It was a successful reintegration week that produced stronger-than-expected signs of adaptable capacity while preserving interpretive discipline.
