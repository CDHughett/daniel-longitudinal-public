## W42 Observation Priorities

### Primary — Repeatable output and recovery cost

- Distinguish **prescribed** from **actual completed** exercises, sets, repetitions, loading, range of motion, tempo and dead-hang time. Where not recorded, preserve missingness instead of supplying implied precision.
- Use W40 and W41 pull/press exposures as distinct comparison doses (5×4 versus 5×5 pull-ups). Do not retroactively describe them as identical repeats.
- Treat the existing hinge/grip prescription as a repeat-exposure reference, not authorization for automatic additional trap-bar volume, weight or hanging time.
- Observe immediate execution **and 24–48-hour delayed response** after higher-priority sessions, including grip, shoulders, lumbar and joint symptoms, soreness, next-day B1 cost, mood, energy and daily activity.
- Monitor sustained/recurrent overnight sleeping-heart-rate elevation and HRV/sleep changes in context; do not let one favorable or unfavorable wearable reading determine an entire training decision.
- Preserve a meaningful distinction among standard, progression, technique/reduced-load and LI rest roles. Do not turn available reserve into mandatory compensatory work.

### Secondary — Comparability and ordinary-life availability

- Continue the familiar approximately 55-minute fasted B1 anchor when context and readiness support it; record actual duration, distance, breathing/heart-rate behavior and departures from typical effort. Seven sessions are not a proof obligation.
- Keep overnight sleep duration separate from consumer wearable stage estimates, REM-confidence labels and subjective dream recall.
- Record standardized waking bodyweight separately from post-B1 and GI-clearance measurements; avoid inferring composition or intake adequacy from acute changes.
- Retain meaningful ordinary-life activity, travel or schedule context when it affects total workload or training opportunity. Absence of LI under a planned rest or valid interruption is not training debt.
- Review dietary adequacy only through source-supported intake, repeated weight, training and recovery observations; do not invent caloric or protein measurements.
- Reconcile any confirmed source-definition conflict through existing governance, not by choosing the most favorable HRV or heart-rate series.
