Later pull-up and grip behavior became trait-like within the alternate configuration.

It remains descriptive.

Autonomic recovery subsequently progressed while normal training continued, but the favorable record 045 autonomic state was not broadly preserved strongly enough across the preregistered August 20–23 scoring window.
