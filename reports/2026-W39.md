## Purpose

This report records the closed 2026-W39 observation window.

Week 39 opened while an out-of-town travel interval was still active. The immediate question was deliberately narrower than progression: after several days without the normal B1 + Load Integration architecture, would ordinary home operation return without make-up volume, protective unloading, motivational rebuilding, or visible reacquisition cost?

That question received a comparatively clean answer.

Travel continued through 2026-09-29. No formal training was performed on either 2026-09-28 or 2026-09-29, and neither day was converted into training debt.

Normal home training resumed on 2026-09-30. Five consecutive complete B1 + Load Integration days then followed through 2026-10-04, totaling 500 formal minutes. Mild initial post-travel stiffness and one transient afternoon tiredness episode were observed on the first return day without changing the prescribed work. By the following day, stiffness had resolved and Load Integration movement setup was again described as automatic.

Week 39 also contained two nights with unusual early-night cardiovascular elevation followed by substantial later normalization. Neither episode was followed by a documented loss of next-day B1, Load Integration, mood, GI/stomach state, pain status, or ordinary function.

Morning bodyweight remained in a lower observed range, but two travel mornings had no scale measurement and measurement context continued to vary. The week therefore preserves the lower observed scale state without assigning body-composition mechanism.

Week 39 closes with a retrospective descriptor of **retention**: after a logistics-directed interruption, the established architecture returned immediately under ordinary home conditions without a rebuilding phase.

This does not establish long-term detraining resistance, repeat the prior reserve probe, authorize progression, or declare a new phase.

The prior reserve/progression question therefore becomes more credible to test, but remains unresolved. Week 39 did not reproduce the Week 37 reserve probe and did not characterize the magnitude or recovery cost of additional workload.

The next useful evidence is a complete ordinary home week under the unchanged prescription.
