## Primary Observation — Return of Comfortable Movement

Track whether ordinary upright posture, comfortable pelvic/lumbar positioning, hinge mechanics, bracing and unloaded movement return without forcing or overriding protective limitation. The objective is not merely to finish a prescribed task; it is to establish the quality and cost of execution.

For trap-bar work, document actual working mass, prescribed versus completed sets/repetitions, pain or stiffness during the movement, position and control, and the subsequent 24–48-hour symptom response. **Do not advance or force heavy hinge loading through restricted or painful mechanics.** With persistent or worsening symptoms, modify/withhold the provocative work and seek appropriate clinical assessment. Escalating neurologic symptoms or bowel/bladder changes require prompt medical evaluation.

Preserved grip and pulling ability should remain separately visible. They do not cancel out a lower-back movement-specific limitation. Likewise, easy B1 work does not independently establish readiness for loaded hinging.
