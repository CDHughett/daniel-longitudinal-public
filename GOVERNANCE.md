# Governance Protocol

The Daniel Longitudinal Study is maintained as a structured archival system.

This repository documents longitudinal biological data under controlled and repeatable conditions.

It is not a marketing vehicle.  
It is not a coaching funnel.  
It is not a social narrative.

All entries are governed by the constraints below.

---

## Core Principles

1. Durability over performance.  
2. Repeatability over peak output.  
3. Normal operating conditions over staged presentation.  
4. Longitudinal trend over isolated result.  
5. Archival integrity over aesthetic impression.  

---

## Capture Constraints

All biometric captures must meet the following conditions:

- No dehydration protocol prior to scan.  
- No sodium manipulation for cosmetic effect.  
- No artificial glycogen depletion.  
- No acute stimulant escalation.  
- No intentional protocol shift solely to influence results.  

If conditions deviate from normal operating parameters, the deviation must be recorded.

---

## Data Integrity

- No retroactive modification of recorded values.  
- No deletion of unfavorable outcomes.  
- No selective reporting.  
- No manipulation of measurement definitions mid-cycle.  

Structural changes to measurement methodology require:
- Documentation in VERSIONING.md  
- Explicit log in CHANGELOG.md  

---

## Interpretation Separation

Raw captures and interpretation are committed separately.

Snapshots reflect data.  
Reports reflect structured analysis.  

Narrative framing is not used as a primary means of interpretation.  
All interpretation must remain artifact-bound and retrospective.  

Language may be used for clarity, but it cannot override, distort, or replace observable data.

---

## Update Cadence

- Snapshots logged upon confirmed capture.  
- Reports added after structured comparison.  
- LATEST.md updated weekly or upon material change.  
- CHANGELOG.md updated with all material commits.  

---

## Scope Boundary

This repository does not:

- Provide medical advice.  
- Recommend interventions.  
- Guarantee outcomes.  
- Optimize for external approval.  

It documents one longitudinal system under constraint.

---

## Permanence Standard

This archive is intended to remain readable and structurally coherent ten years from now.

---

## Artifact Integrity

All binary or non-text snapshot files should be accompanied  
by a `checksums.txt` containing SHA256 hashes for verification.

All additions must satisfy that standard.
