# TELEMETRY

This repository maintains a governed, artifact-first archive of the Daniel Longitudinal Study.

By design, the repository prioritizes structured documentation, retrospective interpretation, stable methodology, and publicly inspectable artifacts. It intentionally excludes most real-time subjective reporting, informal commentary, and transient state observations from the governed archive layer.

A parallel public X account serves as the project’s informal human telemetry layer:

- real-time observations
- subjective state notes
- informal operational reflections
- training and recovery texture
- public-facing commentary around ongoing execution

These observations are not governed in the same way as the repository. They should not be treated as formal dataset entries, validated outcomes, or retrospective reports unless later incorporated into the archive through the repository’s normal documentation process.

The two layers serve different purposes:

- the repository preserves structured, versioned, retrospective artifacts
- the X account preserves unstructured, real-time subjective texture

Together, they provide complementary views of the same long-running observation process.

## Relationship to the Archive

The X account may provide useful context for interpreting subjective state, lived experience, and public-facing operational cadence around the archive.

However, the repository remains the primary source of record for:

- formal reports
- structured data
- snapshot artifacts
- methodology
- governance
- model-error tracking
- archive integrity

Where there is any conflict between informal public commentary and the governed archive, the repository should be treated as the authoritative layer.
