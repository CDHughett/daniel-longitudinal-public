## Why this note exists

The active [2026-W40 report](../reports/2026-W40.md) opened with an explicit plan to complete an unchanged home-condition baseline before reconsidering progression. During the actual observation window, the operator instead executed two defined, submaximal Load Integration progression variants.

The completed exposures are preserved in [the public training records](../data/training_blocks_v1.csv), with one bounded [protocol-context event](../data/context_events_v1.csv). This short record makes the changed prescription legible without rewriting the W40 opening intention, upgrading informal extra repetitions to formally registered tests, or creating a retrospective prediction.

The operator's contemporaneous training instructions and completed private workbook provide source context for what was prescribed and performed. **No separate prospective public protocol document existed before these W40 exposures.** This file was prepared after completion to describe the executed changes and establish reference doses for later comparison. It does not itself authorize a subsequent increase.

## Unchanged reference architecture

The established standard LI prescription at W40 opening was:

- Trap bar: 3 sets × 5 repetitions, controlled tempo at the established working load.
- Strict pull-ups: 4 × 4.
- Submaximal push-ups: 3 × 10 with controlled eccentric and full lockout.
- Dead hangs: 3 × 30 seconds.
- Mobility: approximately 15–20 minutes.
- Nasal incline walk: approximately 10 minutes.

A 55-minute fasted B1 aerobic anchor remained separate from LI. Its prescription was not intentionally expanded by these resistance-training variants.

Actual numeric trap-bar working weight is not specified as a verified absolute load in the W40 structured training field; it remains recorded as **existing load**, not an invented number.

This session changed **multiple** pull/press components. It is not a one-variable isolated test.

## What can and cannot be inferred

**Supported by W40:** Both new variants were completed once; reported global LI RPE remained around 4; no immediate movement failure, pain, or loss of ordinary-day function was documented; standard and reduced-load LI plus the rest boundary remained available.

**Not established by W40:** Repeated-dose tolerance; maximal strength or load ceiling; accumulated connective-tissue cost; superiority of the new dose; adequacy of energy intake under continued progression; attribution of autonomic or sleep changes to the added workload; formal Phase 2D status.

The 2026-W41 observation question is whether comparable exposures, if repeated under appropriate conditions, retain low operating cost and acceptable 24–48-hour recovery. It is **not** a prospective guarantee that those sessions will occur, or an instruction to escalate workload again.
