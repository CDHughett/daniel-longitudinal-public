# Hybrid Expansion Phase (12 Weeks)

## Status

**Historical / Superseded / Not Active**

This document preserves a previously proposed 12-week expansion protocol for provenance.

It does not represent the current operating protocol and should not be interpreted as an active prescription, current training plan, current nutrition target, or authorized progression path.

Current execution remains governed by Phase 2 Load Integration and consolidation principles, including:

- preservation of the existing B1 aerobic anchor
- recovery-compatible Load Integration
- no progression designed to force proof
- no compensatory load escalation
- observation of repeatability, portability, reduced operator overhead, and recovery-floor durability
- protocol modification only after sufficient repeated evidence

The historical material below is retained unchanged in substance so the evolution of protocol design remains auditable.

---

## Historical Phase Objective

Controlled expansion following the conditioning phase.

Historical goals:

- Restore fullness
- Increase strength
- Preserve aerobic base
- Maintain waist stability
- Increase ALMI / FFMI

---

## Historical Rolling 3-Week Structure

### Week A — Load Emphasis

- 1 Heavy Upper session (B4)
- 1 Heavy Lower session (B7)
- 1 Volume Upper session
- 2 B1 aerobic sessions
- 1 B3 recovery-flow session

Historical intake targets:

- Calories: 3,500 kcal
- Protein: 190 g
- Fat: 95 g
- Carbohydrates: 480 g

---

### Week B — Aerobic Bias

- Same general structure
- Heavy lifts reduced by 5–10%
- Slightly longer B1 session

Historical intake targets:

- Calories: 3,400 kcal
- Protein: 190 g
- Fat: 90 g
- Carbohydrates: 440 g

---

### Week C — Consolidation

- Heavy lifts at 3 × 4, approximately RPE 7
- Volume reduced by 20%
- Aerobic work maintained steadily

Historical intake targets:

- Calories: 3,300 kcal
- Protein: 190 g
- Fat: 85–90 g
- Carbohydrates: 410 g

---

## Historical Adjustment Rules

- If waist increased by more than 0.75 inch, reduce intake by 150 kcal across tiers
- If strength stagnated for two or more weeks, add 100 kcal across tiers
- Do not make emotionally reactive adjustments during an active wave

---

## Historical Non-Negotiables

- 2 weekly B1 sessions
- 10,000–15,000 daily steps
- Sauna exposure 3–5 times weekly, without requiring daily use
- Maximum of 1 autophagy-focused day weekly, preferably during Week C

---

## Historical Identity

This was designed as controlled expansion.

It was not intended as:

- a conventional bulk
- a conventional cut

The proposed identity was:

> Hybrid density build.

---

## Current Interpretation

This protocol is preserved as evidence of an earlier proposed direction rather than evidence of completed exposure.

Its calorie targets, training frequency, progression rules, step targets, sauna frequency, and autophagy structure should not be used to explain current biological or performance outcomes unless a separate record establishes that those elements were actually executed during a defined observation window.

No future reactivation is implied.

Any later expansion phase would require:

- a new prospective protocol document
- a clearly defined start date
- explicit governing metrics
- recovery-compatible progression criteria
- separation from historical assumptions in this file
- prospective evaluation rules established before the intervention begins
