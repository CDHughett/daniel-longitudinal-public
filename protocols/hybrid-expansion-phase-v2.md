# Hybrid Expansion Phase (12 Weeks)

## Phase Objective
Controlled expansion following conditioning phase.
Goals:
- Restore fullness
- Increase strength
- Preserve aerobic base
- Maintain waist stability
- Increase ALMI / FFMI

---

# Rolling 3-Week Structure

## Week A – Load Emphasis
- 1 Heavy Upper (B4)
- 1 Heavy Lower (B7)
- 1 Volume Upper
- 2 B1 aerobic sessions
- 1 B3 recovery flow
Calories: 3500 kcal
Protein: 190g
Fat: 95g
Carbs: 480g

---

## Week B – Aerobic Bias
- Same structure
- Heavy lifts reduced 5–10%
- Slightly longer B1 session
Calories: 3400 kcal
Protein: 190g
Fat: 90g
Carbs: 440g

---

## Week C – Consolidation
- Heavy lifts 3x4 @ RPE 7
- Volume reduced 20%
- Aerobic steady
Calories: 3300 kcal
Protein: 190g
Fat: 85–90g
Carbs: 410g

---

# Adjustment Rules
- If waist increases >0.75 inch → reduce 150 kcal across tiers
- If strength stagnates 2+ weeks → add 100 kcal across tiers
- Never adjust mid-wave emotionally

---

# Non-Negotiables
- 2x weekly B1 sessions
- Walking 10–15k steps daily
- Sauna 3–5x weekly (not mandatory daily)
- Max 1 autophagy day weekly (preferably Week C)

---

# Identity
This is controlled expansion.
Not a bulk.
Not a cut.
Hybrid density build.
