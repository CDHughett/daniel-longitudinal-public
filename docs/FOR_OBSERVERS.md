## What You're Looking At

A longitudinal self-tracking archive built around intentionally separated layers:

- **snapshots** → raw artifacts and primary evidence
- **reports** → completed-window retrospective interpretation
- **data** → structured longitudinal datasets
- **model-error layer** → prediction versus observed outcome review
- **governance** → constraints, boundaries, and interpretation rules
- **dashboards** → derived views and current-state orientation

Each layer serves a different role.

The archive should be evaluated by whether those roles remain separated.

## System Constraints

- snapshots are treated as primary evidence
- reports interpret only completed observation windows
- dashboards do not introduce new primary evidence
- datasets preserve structured longitudinal signals
- model-error records are retained rather than removed
- governance constrains interpretation

[ SYSTEM RULES ]

- no forward claims
- no real-time conclusions
- active observations remain provisional
- evaluation is applied retrospectively to completed observation windows
- artifacts precede interpretation
- claims remain bounded by recorded evidence

---

## Why This Exists

Most self-tracking systems collapse evidence, interpretation, and narrative.

When that happens, data becomes story, and story becomes bias.

This archive is designed to reduce that risk by separating:

- what was observed
- what was preserved
- what was interpreted
- what was predicted
- what was later corrected
- what remains uncertain

The archive does not attempt to eliminate error.

It attempts to make error visible.

## If You Are Asking "How Do You Know?"

Most questions about the archive eventually reduce to a single question:

**How does the system determine whether its interpretations are correct?**

Several mechanisms exist for this purpose.

### Prediction Logging

Predictions are documented before outcomes are known.

This creates a record that can later be compared against reality.

### Model Errors

When observations differ from expectations, the discrepancy is documented.

Model errors are retained rather than removed.

They become evidence for future refinement.

### Retrospective Evaluation

Completed observation windows are evaluated only after sufficient evidence has accumulated.

This reduces the risk of real-time narrative construction.

### UDI

The Uncertainty Delta Index evaluates how uncertainty changes as observations accumulate.

The objective is not certainty.

The objective is calibration.

### Concordance

Concordance measures agreement between prediction and outcome.

Higher concordance suggests the model is describing reality more accurately.

Lower concordance suggests revision may be required.

## What To Inspect

A skeptical observer should inspect whether:

- the current state is separated from forward claims
- reports remain retrospective
- artifacts support interpretation
- data coverage is explicit
- model-error records are preserved
- wrong or incomplete predictions are not hidden
- governance constrains interpretation
- subjective telemetry is separated from governed archive records unless formally incorporated

The archive is strongest when it can be inspected from multiple directions and still preserve the same structure.

---

## Boundary

This archive documents one subject under constrained conditions.

It does not claim that observed outcomes generalize to other people.

It does not provide a protocol recommendation.

It does not convert personal observations into universal claims.
