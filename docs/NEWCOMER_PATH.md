# Newcomer Reading Path

If this is your first visit to the archive, start here.

The repository contains several layers built over time.

Reading in the order below will provide the clearest overview.

---

## Step 1 — Understand the Project

Read:

- `README.md`

Purpose:

Understand what the archive is, why it exists, and what it attempts to document.

---

## Step 2 — Understand the Structure

Read:

- `SYSTEM_OVERVIEW.md`

Purpose:

Understand how the repository is organized and how information moves through the archive.

---

## Step 3 — Understand the Data

Read:

- `TELEMETRY.md`

Purpose:

Understand what is being collected and how observations enter the archive.

---

## Step 4 — Understand Error Detection

Read:

- `data/model_error/README.md`

Purpose:

Understand how predictions are evaluated and how model errors are documented.

---

## Step 5 — Understand Uncertainty

Read:

- `docs/methodology/UDI_framework_v1.md`

Purpose:

Understand how uncertainty, prediction performance, and concordance are evaluated.

---

## Step 6 — Explore the Archive

Suggested areas:

- weekly reports
- audit reports
- findings
- model-error records
- longitudinal summaries

At this point most recurring concepts used throughout the archive should be understandable.

For definitions of common terms, see:

- `docs/CONCEPTS.md`
