# Why This Is Public

This repository exists to document a multi-year, self-directed endurance and longevity experiment conducted under stable protocols, consistent logging, and long time horizons.

Most performance work is shared at peaks.

This archive is built to preserve plateaus.

The goal is not inspiration.  
It is signal.

---

## What I’m Actually Studying

This project tracks:

- How slowly compounding training affects physiology over years
- Which biomarkers meaningfully respond to restraint versus novelty
- How protocol stability compares to constant optimization
- What breaks under volume
- What only moves after hundreds of sessions
- How identity, behavior, and biology converge over long horizons

Weekly reports prioritize:

- Training volume summaries
- Experiment outcomes
- Protocol changes (and justifications)
- Testing cycles
- Longitudinal snapshots

---

## Why Publish At All

Publishing publicly removes optionality.

It prevents silent rewrites of history.  
It forces continuity.  
It makes changes legible.  
It makes stagnation visible.  
It creates an external record that cannot be retrofitted after the fact.

This is governance, not marketing.

---

## What Success Looks Like in Five Years

Success is:

- Thousands of logged sessions
- Multiple completed training eras
- Repeated biomarker cycles
- Clear causal inferences
- Protocols that survived boredom
- Mistakes preserved instead of erased
- A dataset that can be audited
- A body of work that outlives any single phase

If the archive is still boring in five years, it worked.
