# RECOVERY MONITORING FRAMEWORK

Recovery integrity governs progression speed.

Load increases are permitted only when recovery markers remain stable.

---

# Daily Monitoring Variables

- Sleep duration
- Sleep consistency
- HRV trend (if available)
- Morning resting heart rate
- Subjective mental clarity
- Muscular soreness
- GI stability

---

# Weekly Monitoring Variables

- Training performance consistency
- Weight trend stability
- Mood consistency
- Endurance tolerance
- Load tolerance

---

# Snapshot-Level Monitoring

- DEXA / Bod Pod recomposition direction
- Lipid panel stability
- Fasting glucose
- Liver enzymes
- Kidney markers

---

# Recovery Status Classification

## GREEN
- Sleep stable
- Performance consistent
- HRV normal range
- No persistent soreness

Action:
→ Load progression allowed

---

## YELLOW
- Mild sleep disruption
- Slight HRV suppression
- Minor soreness accumulation

Action:
→ Hold load constant
→ Reduce endurance volume
→ Monitor for 3–5 days

---

## RED
- Poor sleep trend
- HRV suppression > baseline deviation
- Mood decline
- Joint pain
- Performance regression

Action:
→ Reduce volume 20–40%
→ Remove intensity
→ Increase recovery inputs
→ Reassess after stabilization

---

# Sauna Governance

Sauna exposure must align with:

- Hydration integrity
- Sleep stability
- Load phase intensity

Excessive heat exposure during load escalation is restricted.

---

# Escalation Rule

Load increases only occur when:

- 7+ days of GREEN recovery
- No biomarker regression
- Performance repeatability confirmed

---

# Institutional Reminder

Progression is gated by recovery.

Recovery is the governor of architecture.
