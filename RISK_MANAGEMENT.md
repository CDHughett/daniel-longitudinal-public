# RISK MANAGEMENT

This document defines how systemic risk is identified, assessed, and mitigated within the Daniel Longitudinal system.

The objective is not maximal performance.
The objective is sustainable structural advancement.

---

# Risk Categories

## 1. Mechanical Risk
- Joint overload
- Tendon stress accumulation
- Asymmetry progression
- Rapid load escalation

Mitigation:
- Progressive overload only
- Exposure tracking
- Form audits
- Load ceilings adjusted conservatively

---

## 2. Recovery Risk
- CNS fatigue
- HRV suppression
- Sleep degradation
- Persistent soreness

Mitigation:
- Volume reduction before intensity reduction
- Sauna scaled based on recovery markers
- Deload weeks introduced when trend signals weaken
- No escalation during compromised sleep

---

## 3. Metabolic Risk
- Fasting glucose drift
- Lipid panel regression
- Excessive caloric deficit
- Chronic underfueling during load phase

Mitigation:
- Regular bloodwork snapshots
- Refeed calibration
- Caloric increases tied to load expansion
- No prolonged deficit during load amplification

---

## 4. Hormonal Risk
- Overtraining suppression
- Libido suppression
- Chronic sympathetic dominance

Mitigation:
- Load increase capped by recovery markers
- Biomarker checks during escalations
- Avoid concurrent stress stacking (life + load)

---

## 5. Narrative Risk

Public presentation must not:
- Overstate results
- Predict unconfirmed outcomes
- Introduce aspirational phase declarations

Mitigation:
- Snapshot confirmation before narrative shift
- Conservative framing
- Governance adherence

---

# Escalation Protocol

If 2+ risk markers appear simultaneously:

1. Reduce volume.
2. Maintain movement quality.
3. Prioritize sleep.
4. Delay progression.
5. Reassess after 7 days.

No escalation during instability.

---

# Institutional Standard

If a decision improves short-term metrics but increases systemic fragility, it is rejected.

Durability overrides velocity.
