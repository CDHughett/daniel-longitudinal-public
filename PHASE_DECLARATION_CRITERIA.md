# PHASE DECLARATION CRITERIA

Phases are not motivational labels.
They are retrospective architectural confirmations.

A phase is declared only after meeting the following criteria.

---

# 1. Structural Shift Confirmed

A new phase requires:

- A measurable shift in dominant stimulus bias
- A change in training architecture
- A defined system-level constraint being addressed

Example:
Endurance bias → Load density bias

---

# 2. Snapshot Evidence Present

At least one confirmed snapshot must show:

- Directional physiological change
- Stabilized biomarker trend
- Mechanical capacity shift

Subjective progress alone is insufficient.

---

# 3. Repeatability Demonstrated

The new structure must:

- Persist across multiple weeks
- Show adaptation without regression
- Maintain recovery integrity

One strong week does not justify a phase declaration.

---

# 4. Previous Phase Completion

The outgoing phase must demonstrate:

- Its intended objective met
- Its limiting factor identified
- Its structural stability achieved

Phases end by fulfillment, not boredom.

---

# 5. Risk Assessment Conducted

Before declaring a new phase:

- Overtraining risk assessed
- Biomarker stress evaluated
- Hormonal impact considered
- Recovery trend verified

Escalation without stability is prohibited.

---

# 6. Retrospective Framing Only

Phases are declared only after:

- Evidence exists
- Trends are verified
- Structure is confirmed

Future phases are never declared aspirationally.

---

# Declaration Format

When a phase is declared, the following artifacts must exist:

- PHASE_CLOSEOUT_SUMMARY.md (previous phase)
- Updated PHASE_INDEX.md
- Updated EPOCH_MAP.md
- Documented structural bias shift

---

# Governance Standard

If criteria are partially met, the system remains in the current phase.

Conservatism protects integrity.
